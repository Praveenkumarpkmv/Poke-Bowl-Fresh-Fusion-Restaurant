ABOUT US · FRESH SINCE 2024

A bowl full of honest food

We're a small team with a big obsession — making fresh, colorful, fusion-inspired bowls that taste as good as they look. No shortcuts, no compromises, just real food made with care.

OUR VALUES

What We Stand For

More than a restaurant — we're a promise. Every bowl we serve is built on four values that guide every decision we make.

Value 01

Fresh, Never Frozen

Every ingredient is delivered daily from local farms and fisheries. If it's not fresh, it's not on our menu.

Value 02

Community First

We partner with local farmers, support neighborhood events, and give back to food banks.

03

Zero Waste Goal

Compostable packaging, a strict zero-food-waste program, and ocean-friendly sourcing across our entire supply chain.

Value 04

Craft Over Speed

We take the time to marinate, ferment, and hand-cut. Every bowl is assembled with intention — never rushed.

Our Promise

"We don't just serve food — we serve a promise. Fresh ingredients, honest sourcing, and a bowl made with care every single time."

— The Poke Bowl & Fresh Fusion Team

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From a tiny farmers market stall to a beloved neighborhood spot — here's a look at our story.

'22

The First Stall

Started at the local farmers market with just three bowl recipes, a folding table, and a big dream.

'23

First Storefront

Opened our first brick-and-mortar location in the heart of the city — 40 seats, one open kitchen, endless bowls.

'24

Flavor Passport Launch

Introduced our globally-inspired menu with 12 signature sauces — a culinary trip around the world in a bowl.

'26

50,000 Bowls Served

Celebrating a major milestone with the community that made it all possible. Here's to the next 50k!
