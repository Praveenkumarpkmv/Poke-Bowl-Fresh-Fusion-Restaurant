BUILD YOUR BOWL · MADE TO ORDER

Four steps. Infinite bowls.

Pick a base, choose your protein, pile on the toppings, and finish with a signature sauce. Over 30 fresh ingredients — the combinations are entirely up to you.

How It Works

Your Bowl, In Four Moves

No menus to memorize, no wrong answers. Just a simple, four-step flow from base to bowl.

01

Choose Your Base

Sushi rice, brown rice, mixed greens, quinoa, or zucchini noodles. The foundation of your bowl.

02

Pick a Protein

Ahi tuna, salmon, spicy tuna, marinated tofu, grilled chicken, or shrimp — all cut fresh.

03

Stack the Toppings

Avocado, edamame, mango, seaweed, crispy onions — pile on as many as you like.

04

Finish With Sauce

Twelve signature sauces — from citrus ponzu to black garlic sesame. Pick one, or two.

Built In Front Of You

Watch your bowl come together, layer by layer.

Every bowl is assembled fresh the moment you order. No prep trays, no reheating — just a clean counter, a sharp knife, and a bowl that's yours from the first scoop to the last.

  • All ingredients kept at peak freshness — never sitting out
  • Portion sizes you control, so every bowl fits your appetite
  • Swap, skip, or double any topping at no extra charge
  • Gluten-free, vegan, and low-carb options available for every step
