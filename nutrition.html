Dietary Options

Eat The Way You Want

We've built our menu so nearly every diet can find a bowl that works. Here's how each option fits.

Vegan

Plant-based proteins, veggie bases, and dairy-free sauces. Over 15 vegan combinations available.

Gluten-Free

Most of our bases and proteins are naturally gluten-free. Tamari replaces soy on request.

High Protein

Double protein, choose lean options, and skip rice for greens — up to 45g per bowl.

Low Sodium

Ask for no added sauce and skip pickled items. Bowls can drop below 400mg sodium.

Low Carb

Swap rice for mixed greens or zucchini noodles and load up on protein and veggies.

Keto-Friendly

Zucchini noodle base, high-protein toppings, healthy fats, and no-sugar sauces.
