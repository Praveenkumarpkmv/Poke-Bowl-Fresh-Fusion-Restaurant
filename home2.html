Our Philosophy

The Fusion Approach

We don't just make poke bowls — we create experiences that blend cultures, techniques, and flavors.

Global Inspiration

From Japanese precision to Hawaiian soul, Korean heat to Mediterranean freshness — we draw from culinary traditions around the world.

Signature Sauces

The Sauces That Define Us

Twelve house-made sauces, each crafted to elevate your bowl. Here's a closer look at our six most-loved blends.

01 Bright & Tangy

Citrus Ponzu

Yuzu, soy, mirin, and a touch of rice vinegar. The perfect balance of sweet, salty, and tart that lets fresh seafood sing.

Best with: Ahi Tuna & Salmon
02 Spicy & Creamy

Sriracha Aioli

House mayo, sriracha, garlic, and fresh lime. Creamy heat that complements every protein without overpowering.

Best with: Spicy Salmon & Shrimp
03 Nutty & Aromatic

Sesame Ginger

Toasted sesame, grated ginger, tamari, and wild honey. Warm, nutty, and deeply savory with rich umami.

Best with: Warm Grains & Greens
04 Rich & Umami

Tahini Miso

Creamy stone-ground tahini, white miso, lemon, and maple. Earthy, velvety, and deeply satisfying.

Best with: Crispy Tofu & Sweet Potato
05 Zesty & Bold

Yuzu Kosho

Fermented yuzu peel, fiery green chili, and sea salt. A punchy, citrus-forward kick with gentle building heat.

Best with: Seared Scallops & Yellowfin
06 Deep & Savory

Black Garlic Sesame

Slow-roasted fermented black garlic, toasted sesame, and tamari. Rich, molasses-like depth with intense umami.

Best with: Forbidden Rice & Steak
