Our Philosophy

The Fusion Approach

We don't just make poke bowls — we create experiences that blend cultures, techniques, and flavors.

Global Inspiration

From Japanese precision to Hawaiian soul, Korean heat to Mediterranean freshness — we draw from culinary traditions around the world.

Modern Technique

Sous-vide proteins, house-fermented sauces, and precise knife work ensure every bowl is consistent and exceptional.

Local Love

We source from local farms and fisheries whenever possible, supporting our community and reducing our footprint.

Signature Sauces

The Sauces That Define Us

Twelve house-made sauces, each crafted to elevate your bowl. Here's a closer look at our four most-loved blends.

01

Bright & Tangy

Citrus Ponzu

Yuzu, soy, mirin, and a touch of rice vinegar. The perfect balance of sweet, salty, and tart.

02

Spicy & Creamy

Sriracha Aioli

House mayo, sriracha, garlic, and lime. Creamy heat that complements every protein.

03

Nutty & Aromatic

Sesame Ginger

Toasted sesame, fresh ginger, soy, and honey. Warm, nutty, and deeply savory.

04

Rich & Umami

Tahini Miso

Creamy tahini, white miso, lemon, and maple. Earthy, umami-rich, and unforgettable.

05

Zesty & Bold

Yuzu Kosho

Fermented yuzu peel, green chili, and sea salt. A punchy, citrus-forward kick with gentle heat.

06

Deep & Savory

Black Garlic Sesame

Slow-roasted black garlic, toasted sesame, and tamari. Rich, molasses-like depth with umami.
